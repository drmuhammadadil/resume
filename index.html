Classical Islamic law, read for the questions of the present

Dr. Muhammad Adil is a Lecturer in the Department of Islamic Studies at Bacha Khan University, Charsadda, with a research focus on Seerah, Islamic jurisprudence, and the intersection of classical Islamic law with international humanitarian law. His work examines how the Prophetic tradition speaks to the modern ethics of armed conflict and the protection of civilians.

He has authored eight research-based books and published thirty-two papers in HEC-recognized journals, presented twelve papers at international conferences, and received Presidential honors alongside repeated recognition from the ICRC and NACTA.
