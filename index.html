About

A scholarly path through Seerah and law

"His research treats the life of the Prophet ﷺ not as history to be recounted, but as law to be applied."

Dr. Muhammad Adil holds a PhD in Islamic Studies from Abdul Wali Khan University, Mardan (2018), and completed post-doctoral research at the International Research Institute, International Islamic University, Islamabad (2026). He has taught in the Department of Islamic Studies at Bacha Khan University, Charsadda since 2022.

His work has been recognized with Presidential honors from Pakistan's Ministry of Religious Affairs, and repeated awards from the International Committee of the Red Cross (ICRC) and the National Counter Terrorism Authority (NACTA) for essays on Islam, humanitarian law, and the protection of civilians in armed conflict.
